Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the JinnFiles iOS application (“JinnFiles”, “we”, “our” or “the app”) handles information when you use the app.

1. Information we access

If you choose to sign in with Google, JinnFiles receives basic profile information made available through Google sign-in, such as your name, email address and profile image. The app requests Google Drive access so that you can use its Drive file-management features.

Depending on the actions you take, JinnFiles accesses Drive file and folder information and file contents needed to browse, search, create folders, rename, move, copy, upload, download or delete items. The current app implementation requests the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive, which permits broad access to Google Drive. You should only grant this permission if you are comfortable with the access described in Google's consent screen.

2. How information is used

Google profile information is used to identify the signed-in account within the app. Google Drive data is used to provide the Drive features you initiate. JinnFiles does not use Google user data for advertising, sell it, or use it to build data profiles unrelated to the app's file-management functions.

3. Where information is processed and stored

The app communicates directly with Google services, including Google's OAuth token endpoint, Google account user-info endpoint and Google Drive API, to authenticate you and perform requested Drive operations. The source code supplied for review does not show a JinnFiles-operated backend server receiving or storing Google Drive file contents.

Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device. The app attempts to store token data in iOS Keychain; if that operation fails, the current implementation falls back to storing the token data in the app's UserDefaults storage. Basic signed-in profile information is also cached locally in the app. Files you choose to upload are transferred to the Google Drive account and destination you select. Files you download are saved to a location you choose on your device.

4. Sharing and disclosure

JinnFiles sends information to Google when necessary to complete Google sign-in and the Drive operations you request. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy. The app's reviewed source does not show a separate JinnFiles server or an advertising/analytics SDK receiving Google Drive data. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

We do not sell Google user data or disclose it to third parties for advertising. We do not allow humans to read Google user data except where necessary to provide support that you request, comply with applicable law, or address security issues, and only as permitted by applicable Google policies.

5. Data retention and deletion

Authentication tokens and cached account profile information remain on your device until you sign out, remove the app's stored data, or uninstall the app (subject to iOS storage and Keychain behavior). When you use the app's sign-out function, JinnFiles attempts to revoke the refresh token with Google and removes the locally stored token and cached profile data.

Files uploaded to Google Drive remain in your Google account until you move or delete them. Deleting a file through JinnFiles affects the corresponding item in your Google Drive. You can also manage or delete your Google data directly through Google Drive and your Google Account.

6. Security

JinnFiles uses Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization flow with PKCE and communicates with Google endpoints over HTTPS. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Because the current implementation includes a UserDefaults fallback for tokens when Keychain storage fails, we do not claim that token data is always stored exclusively in Keychain.

7. Your choices and Google permissions

You may choose not to connect a Google Account. You can revoke JinnFiles' access at any time through your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access may prevent Google Drive features from working until you authorize the app again.

8. Children's privacy

JinnFiles is not designed to collect personal information from children, and we do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
